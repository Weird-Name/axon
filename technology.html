CONCEPT & FEASIBILITY

Concept Stage: Requirements Definition, Multi-Physics Modeling & Feasibility Analysis

We establish the technical foundation of new product and system developments through feasibility studies, requirement analysis, preliminary engineering assessments, and performance modelling to translate concepts into technically viable solutions.

Requirements Synthesis Multi-Physics Simulation Feasibility Sizing 3D Space-Claim Optimization
  • ✓ Comprehensive Requirements Synthesis: Defining operational duty cycles, environmental boundaries, load dynamics, and regulatory targets across electro-mechanical, thermal, and process domains.
  • ✓ Multi-Physics & Energy Balances: Preliminary analytical calculations, power-to-weight optimization, thermal dissipation modeling, and lifecycle energy efficiency projections.
  • ✓ System Architecture & Subsystem Sizing: Sizing core modules, component selection matrices, and material compatibility envelopes against harsh duty cycles.
  • ✓ Preliminary 3D Space-Claim Benchmarking: Early spatial packaging, structural interface envelopes, ergonomic access analysis, and modular layout integration.
  • ✓ Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) & Risk Assessment: Comprehensive techno-economic trade-off matrices, Capex/Opex modeling, and initial Design Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (DFMEA).

DETAILED ENGINEERING & SIMULATION

Design Stage: Multi-Discipline 3D CAD, FEA Structural Stress & CFD Flow Optimization

We deliver integrated engineering design solutions, transforming defined requirements into optimized architectures, detailed technical specifications, engineering drawings, and design configurations, with a focus on functionality, reliability, and performance.

Parametric 3D CAD FEA Structural Analysis CFD Thermal & Fluid Functional Safety Architecture
  • ✓ High-Precision 3D CAD Modeling: Multi-discipline assembly models, sub-assembly component layouts, tolerance stack-up analysis, and detailed manufacturing drawings.
  • ✓ Finite Element Analysis (FEA): Static, cyclic fatigue, vibration modal analysis, and transient mechanical shock evaluations under maximum working loads.
  • ✓ Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD): Multi-phase fluid dynamics, aerodynamic/hydrodynamic drag optimization, pressure loss reduction, and conjugate heat transfer analysis.
  • ✓ System Safety & Reliability Engineering: HAZOP/HAZID studies, quantitative risk modeling, DFMEA, and functional safety compliance (SIL / ASIL rating).
  • ✓ Global Code & Standards Alignment: Engineering verified strictly against ASME, ISO, IEC, IEEE, TEMA, API, and UNECE regulatory requirements.

Prototyping Stage: Precision Fabrication, Subsystem Assembly & Laboratory Trials

We facilitate the realization of engineering designs through prototype development, system integration, and functional implementation, enabling design refinement and early-stage assessment of technical feasibility.

5-Axis CNC Machining Qualified Welding (WPS/PQR) Pilot Subsystem Assembly Hardware-In-The-Loop (HIL)
  • ✓ Rapid Precision Fabrication: Fast-turnaround 5-axis CNC machining, laser cutting, precision forming, and additive manufacturing for rapid proof-of-concept components.
  • ✓ Certified Welding & Joining: Joining in accordance with Axon's 26 qualified ASME IX and AWS D1.1 WPS/PQR procedures across carbon steel, SS 316L, and exotic alloys.
  • ✓ Subsystem Integration & Harnessing: Assembly of pilot mechanical frames, liquid thermal loops, high-voltage wiring harnesses, and custom electronic control modules.
  • ✓ Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) & Dynamometer Testing: Characterization on dynamic test benches, electronic load banks, and thermal conditioning chambers.
  • ✓ Pre-Compliance Bench Screening: Preliminary radiated and conducted emissions (EMI/EMC), structural vibration response, and thermal boundary verification.

DESIGN VERIFICATION & RELIABILITY

Validation Stage: Design Verification Testing (DVT), Environmental Stress & NDT

We execute structured verification and validation activities through engineering analysis, functional testing, and performance assessments to establish compliance with design specifications, operational requirements, and quality benchmarks.

Design Verification Testing (DVT) 100% NDT Inspection Climatic Chamber Shock Multi-Axis Vibration
  • ✓ Advanced Non-Destructive Testing (NDT): Radiographic (RT), Ultrasonic (UT), Magnetic Particle (MPI), and Dye Penetrant (DPT) inspections for total joint integrity.
  • ✓ Proof Pressure & Structural Load Testing: Hydrostatic proof testing up to 1.5x design pressure and mass-spectrometer leak tests verifying zero emissions.
  • ✓ Environmental Climatic Stress Screening: Thermal cycling from -40°C to +85°C, accelerated corrosion salt-fog exposure, and high-humidity endurance.
  • ✓ Multi-Axis Dynamic Vibration & Shock: Shaker table random vibration and mechanical shock profiles simulating decades of field service life.
  • ✓ Full-Load Continuous Endurance Runs: Continuous endurance trials verifying thermal saturation margins, power delivery efficiency, and control software stability.
