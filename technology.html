STAGE 01 // 07 CONCEPT STAGE

Concept Stage: Requirements Definition, Calculations & Performance Estimations

During the concept stage of a new product or system, we help identify key features and technical requirements, providing multi-physics calculations, powertrain sizing, and performance estimations to de-risk investment before detailed engineering.

Duty Cycle Analysis Power & Torque Sizing 3D Space-Claim TCO Feasibility
  • ✓ Identification of client operating envelopes, duty cycles, and payload/speed requirements.
  • ✓ Multi-physics preliminary calculations, power-to-weight optimization, and range/energy estimations.
  • ✓ Powertrain component sizing: motor continuous/peak torque, inverter DC voltage, and battery kWh capacity.
  • ✓ Vehicle packaging space-claim benchmarking and preliminary 3D CAD envelope integration.
  • ✓ Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) modeling and initial feasibility risk matrix.

STAGE 02 // 07 DESIGN STAGE

Design Stage: Automotive Standards, Functional Safety, Cybersecurity & Multi-Physics CAD

Conducted using established automotive and industrial engineering standards. We integrate FMEA risk mitigation, ISO 26262 functional safety up to ASIL D, and ISO 21434 automotive cybersecurity from the very first CAD sketch and electrical schematic.

ISO 26262 (ASIL D) ISO 21434 Cybersecurity DFMEA / PFMEA 3D CAD / FEA / CFD
  • ✓ System & Design FMEA (DFMEA / PFMEA) to systematically identify and mitigate failure modes.
  • ✓ ISO 26262 Functional Safety architecture: HARA, safety goals, and ASIL D hardware/software metrics.
  • ✓ ISO 21434 Automotive Cybersecurity: Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA) for connected ECUs.
  • ✓ High-precision 3D CAD modeling, FEA mechanical stress analysis, and CFD thermal/fluid cooling simulations.
  • ✓ Schematic electrical design, high-voltage wiring architecture, and PCB electronic hardware layouts.

STAGE 03 // 07 PROTOTYPING STAGE

Prototyping Stage: Rapid Fabrication, Subsystem Assembly & In-House Laboratory Bench Trials

Product prototypes are engineered, fabricated, and evaluated rapidly. Utilizing our comprehensive in-house laboratory testing bays, we evaluate performance, electrical efficiency, thermal equilibrium, and EMI/EMC parameters against target specifications.

Rapid CNC & ASME Welding HV Wire Harnessing Dyno Motor Calibration Pre-Compliance EMI
  • ✓ Fast-turnaround CNC 5-axis machining, laser plate cutting, and certified ASME IX / AWS D1.1 welding.
  • ✓ High-voltage wiring harness assembly, automated crimping, and custom PCB pilot manufacturing.
  • ✓ Battery pack pilot assembly featuring laser-welded busbars and active liquid thermal cold-plates.
  • ✓ In-house test bay evaluation: dynamometer motor characterization and inverter switching efficiency curves.
  • ✓ Pre-compliance EMI/EMC screening to evaluate radiated emissions and signal integrity.

STAGE 04 // 07 VALIDATION STAGE

Validation Stage: Environmental Stress, Dyno Endurance & Rigorous Standards Compliance

Individual components, power electronics, and fully integrated vehicle powertrains undergo rigorous validation under extreme environmental stress and extended duty cycles to ensure strict compliance with international standards before tooling freeze.

-40°C to +85°C Thermal Shock Multi-Axis Vibration Shaker IP67 / IP6K9K Ingress HIL Fault Injection
  • ✓ Thermal chamber stress testing: cyclic thermal shock from -40°C to +85°C and high-humidity endurance.
  • ✓ Multi-axis shaker table vibration and mechanical shock simulating 1,000,000+ km of rugged commercial usage.
  • ✓ High-voltage electrical safety testing: dielectric withstand breakdown, isolation monitoring, and IP67/IP6K9K ingress protection.
  • ✓ Full-load dynamometer endurance runs verifying continuous torque, thermal saturation, and regenerative braking.
  • ✓ Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) automated fault injection, short-circuit protection, and fail-safe recovery validation.

STAGE 05 // 07 HOMOLOGATION STAGE

Homologation Stage: UN/ECE Type Approval, Regulatory Compliance & Vehicle Certification

In close collaboration with accredited international technical services and regulatory authorities, we provide end-to-end homologation support including UN ECE R-100, ECE R-85, and ECE R-10 certifications alongside complete vehicle integration sign-off.

ECE R-100 (EV Safety) ECE R-85 (Net Power) ECE R-10 (EMC) TÜV / ARAI Homologation
  • ✓ UN ECE R-100 Type Approval: Complete electrical powertrain safety, isolation resistance, and REESS battery compliance.
  • ✓ UN ECE R-85 Certification: Official net power and 30-minute maximum continuous power rating verification.
  • ✓ UN ECE R-10 EMC Type Approval: Comprehensive electromagnetic compatibility testing in accredited anechoic chambers.
  • ✓ Preparation of exhaustive regulatory documentation dossiers, test reports, and technical descriptions.
  • ✓ Direct liaison with certification authorities (TÜV, UTAC, IDIADA, ARAI) through official Certificate of Conformity (CoC).

Series Production: Robotized Assembly Bays, IATF 16949 Quality & 100% End-of-Line Testing

IATF 16949 Automotive ISO 9001:2015 100% Automated EOL 2D DataMatrix Traceability
  • ✓ Advanced automated assembly lines with robotic laser welding and precision torque-angle fastening.
  • ✓ IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 quality management with Statistical Process Control (SPC) across all stations.
