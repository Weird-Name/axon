GROUP OVERVIEW & STRATEGIC REACH

Two Decades of Heavy Engineering & Strategic Industrial Transformation

Headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with established international operations across Malaysia and Singapore, Axon Petro Engineering Pvt. Ltd. operates across high-growth strategic business verticals—including Turnkey EPC, Sustainable Mobility, HVAC & Thermal Engineering, and Global Material Trading—anchored by our proprietary Centre of Excellence on Advance Engineering Research.

In strategic joint venture with Greengold Energy (JV), our engineering teams have executed landmark industrial plants and multi-thousand-ton structural projects for international clients including PETRONAS, Emery Oleochemicals, LANXESS, Synthomer, Yokogawa, and Foster Wheeler with an unblemished zero-LTI safety track record.
