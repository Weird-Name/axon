SUB-VERTICAL 02

Organic & Chemical Fertilizers

Native Indian manufacturer of 100% verified organic liquid bio-nutrients reviewed and approved by USA advisory groups. High-shear reactor blending and precision drone fertigation.

  • Humic Active Complex (45%–60%) for high cation exchange
  • Balanced Carbon to Nitrogen Ratio (C:N < 15:1)
  • Heavy metals mitigation tested far below safety limits
  • High agronomic impact on Palm, Orchards, Sugarcane & Cotton
  • Precision foliar spray via micro-drip & agricultural UAV drones

SUB-VERTICAL 03

All Types of Commodities

Pure-play asset trading, processing facilitation, and bulk supply chains for energy transition bio-feedstocks (Used Cooking Oil for HVO/SAF), culinary edible oils, sugar, and rice.

  • Used Cooking Oil (UCO) with certified ISCC & SGS audit trail
  • Crude & Refined Sunflower, Palm (CPO/RBD), and Soybean Oils
  • Refined Cane Sugar (ICUMSA 45) with >99.8% polarization
  • Premium aromatic Indian Basmati & Parboiled Rice
  • Flexitanks (24,000L), ISO-Tanks, and intermodal transport
