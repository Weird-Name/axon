THERMAL SYSTEMS & INDUSTRIAL HVAC EXCHANGERS • WHRU • AIR HANDLING

Engineered Thermal Solutions for Severe Duty

Engineered specifically for oil refineries, chemical plants, and extreme ambient industrial facilities, our thermal systems optimize energy recovery while safeguarding operations against explosive atmospheres.

  • ✓ District Cooling & Chilled Water: Centralized cooling distribution reducing industrial power consumption and equipment footprint.
  • ✓ Hazardous Zone AHUs (ATEX/IECEx): Explosion-proof certified fan motors, positive pressure airlocks, and chemical filtration.
  • ✓ TEMA Heat Exchangers: Class R, C, and B shell & tube units designed with HTRI thermal modeling and zero cross-contamination.
  • ✓ Waste Heat Recovery Units (WHRU): Extracting heat from boiler exhaust, incinerators, and turbine flues to generate process steam.
  • ✓ Large-Bore Heavy Ducting: Heavy-gauge stainless steel (304L/316L) ducting, expansion joints, and flanged chimney stacks.
