OUR SPECIALIZED OFFERINGS INCLUDE

Specialized HVAC & Cooling Solutions

Engineered cooling, precision climate management, and air decontamination systems designed to optimize energy efficiency and maintain critical operational parameters.

Industrial Cooling Solutions

Design, engineering, and turnkey installation of centralized chiller plants (water-cooled and air-cooled), heavy industrial cooling towers, and closed-loop process fluid chillers. Sized for continuous 24/7 duty cycles and high ambient factory environments.

  • Water-cooled & air-cooled central chiller plants
  • Cooling tower pumping networks & condenser piping
  • Process fluid refrigeration & heat rejection skids
  • Energy audits for lower kW/TR power consumption
CHILLERS • PROCESS COOLING

Industrial Ventilation Solutions

Engineered ventilation networks ensuring ambient workforce comfort and regulatory environmental safety. Incorporates forced fresh-air supply, steam/smoke evacuation, toxic fume extraction, and two-stage evaporative cooling via industrial air washers.

  • Industrial air washers & evaporative cooling systems
  • Factory fresh-air induction & positive pressure flow
  • Process steam, chemical fume & welding exhaust
  • Heavy-gauge galvanized and stainless steel ductwork
VENTILATION • AIR WASHERS

Health Care HVAC Solutions

Mission-critical infection control and sterile climate systems for hospitals, pharmaceutical facilities, and medical cleanrooms. Built strictly to ISO 14644 cleanroom classes with laminar airflow plenums and multi-tier HEPA filtration.

  • Modular Operation Theatre (OT) climate systems
  • Positive & negative pressure isolation ward rooms
  • HEPA H13/H14 terminal ceiling filter housings
  • NABH and cGMP regulatory air qualification
CLEANROOMS • HOSPITALS

Air Disinfection

Advanced ultraviolet germicidal irradiation (UV-C) and active air disinfection fixtures engineered to neutralize airborne viruses, bacteria, and mold spores inside air distribution ducts and indoor public environments.

  • In-duct UV-C germicidal coil irradiation systems
  • Upper-room UV air disinfection for occupied spaces
  • Elimination of biofilm on AHU cooling coils
  • Odor control, volatile organic compound (VOC) removal
UVC DISINFECTION • GERMICIDAL

Comfort Cooling Solutions

As an Authorized Channel Partner of Daikin, we supply and commission next-generation Variable Refrigerant Volume (VRV / VRF) multi-split systems, packaged air conditioners, and concealed ducted solutions for offices and retail developments.

  • Authorized Daikin VRV / VRF multi-split systems
  • Ductable and packaged commercial air conditioning
  • Ceiling-mounted cassette units & multi-zone controls
  • High-efficiency inverter compressors reducing energy bills
DAIKIN VRV / VRF PARTNER

Data Centre Cooling Solutions

High-precision cooling architecture engineered for dense server rooms, control rooms, and data centres (e.g. Reserve Bank of India server networks). High sensible heat ratio (SHR > 0.90) and active N+1 redundancy ensure zero thermal downtime.

  • Precision Air Conditioning (PAC) units for 24/7 service
  • Tight temperature (20°C–22°C) and relative humidity control
  • Hot aisle / cold aisle containment integration
  • Active telemetry, alarm logging, and dual-circuit backup
PRECISION AIR CONDITIONING (PAC)

Air Quality Improvement

Comprehensive indoor air quality (IAQ) engineering combining multi-tier particulate filtration, automated CO2 fresh-air modulation, and carbon chemical filtering to ensure healthy, productive, and compliant industrial/commercial environments.

  • Multi-stage pre-filters, micro-vee, and carbon filters
  • Continuous indoor air quality sensors & CO2 telemetry
  • Automated demand-controlled ventilation (DCV)
  • ASHRAE 62.1 indoor ventilation rate compliance
INDOOR AIR QUALITY • IAQ
TURNKEY PROJECT EXECUTION

Offering Complete HVAC Turnkey Services

End-to-end execution capability managing every phase of project delivery under single-point engineering responsibility.

Project Design

Comprehensive heat load calculations (ASHRAE/ISHRAE standards), AutoCAD layouts, 3D BIM coordination, and detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) preparation.

Equipment Supply

Direct procurement of Daikin VRV/VRF equipment, chillers, precision PAC units, custom air handling units, fans, cooling towers, and certified valves.

Site Installation

Precision sheet-metal duct fabrication, refrigerant and chilled-water piping, acoustic insulation, electrical cabling, and smart sensor placement.

Testing & Commissioning

Testing, Adjusting & Balancing (TAB), air velocity testing, hydrostatic proof tests, automated control sequence tuning, and final client handover.
