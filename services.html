STAGE 01 // 07 CONCEPT STAGE

Concept Stage: Operating Parameters, Multi-Physics Calculations, Heat & Mass Balances & Feasibility Sizing

During the concept phase of an industrial facility, thermal network, or clean mobility project, we establish fundamental technical envelopes, process flow duties, and thermodynamic calculations to de-risk investment and optimize lifecycle Capex/Opex.

Process Flow & HMB ASME & ISO Sizing Thermodynamic Feasibility 3D Spatial Envelopes
  • ✓ Client technical requirements definition across chemical processes, thermal loads, operating pressures, and duty cycles.
  • ✓ Multi-physics preliminary calculations, heat & mass balances (HMB), power-to-weight ratios, and energy efficiency projections.
  • ✓ Equipment sizing: ASME pressure vessels, reactors, industrial AHUs, shell & tube exchangers, and electric powertrain ratings.
  • ✓ Preliminary 3D CAD space-claim benchmarking, plant layout integration, and footprint optimization.
  • ✓ Comprehensive Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) modeling, Capex/Opex trade-off matrices, and initial safety risk assessment.

STAGE 02 // 07 DESIGN STAGE

Design Stage: Multi-Discipline 3D CAD, FEA Mechanical Stress, CFD Thermal Flow & International Codes

Conducted using established international engineering codes (ASME, TEMA, API, ISO, UNECE). We integrate comprehensive hazard mitigation, HAZOP process safety, FMEA, and functional safety architecture from the very first CAD model and P&ID schematic.

ASME Sec VIII / B31.3 ISO 26262 / IEC 61508 HAZOP / DFMEA 3D CAD / FEA / CFD
  • ✓ Detailed Process & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs), high-voltage electrical single-lines, and control logic diagrams.
  • ✓ High-precision 3D CAD modeling, piping isometric generation, and multi-axis structural steelwork layouts.
  • ✓ Multi-physics Finite Element Analysis (FEA) for mechanical stress and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) for fluid and thermal dissipation.
  • ✓ HAZOP (Hazard and Operability Study), DFMEA / PFMEA, and functional safety compliance (SIL 3 / ASIL D metrics).
  • ✓ Full code compliance design under ASME Section VIII Div 1 & 2, ASME B31.3/B31.1, TEMA, API 650, ATEX/IECEx, and UNECE standards.
Design Stage Technical Execution STAGE 02
STAGE 03 // 07 PROTOTYPING STAGE

Prototyping Stage: Precision CNC Machining, Certified ASME Welding, Pilot Skid Assembly & Test Rig Trials

Prototypes, pilot process skids, and powertrain modules are engineered and fabricated rapidly. Utilizing precision CNC machine centers, certified welding bays, and in-house laboratory test rigs, we evaluate mechanical integrity, flow dynamics, and electrical performance against target criteria.

ASME IX & AWS D1.1 Welding 5-Axis Precision CNC Pilot Skid Assembly Electrical & Hydro Testing
  • ✓ Fast-turnaround 5-axis CNC precision machining, plasma/laser plate profiling, and heavy plate rolling.
  • ✓ Certified welding under Axon's 26 qualified ASME IX and AWS D1.1 WPS/PQR procedures across CS, SS 316L, Super Duplex, and Hastelloy.
  • ✓ Pilot skid fabrication: chemical dosing skids, hazardous-area AHU housings, heat exchanger test bundles, and high-voltage harnesses.
  • ✓ Pilot assembly of custom electronics, Vehicle Control Units (VCU), battery thermal cold-plates, and high-pressure manifold blocks.
  • ✓ In-house laboratory bench testing: hydrostatic leak checks, dynamometer efficiency curves, and pre-compliance EMI/EMC screening.
Prototyping Stage Technical Execution STAGE 03
STAGE 04 // 07 VALIDATION STAGE

Validation Stage: Hydrostatic Proof Tests, 100% NDT Inspection, Environmental Stress & Full-Load Endurance

Pressure equipment, industrial HVAC units, and vehicle powertrains undergo rigorous stress testing under extreme operating envelopes and environmental conditions to guarantee zero-failure reliability before field commissioning or series deployment.

Hydrostatic Proof Testing 100% NDT (RT/UT/MPI) -40°C to +85°C Thermal Shock Multi-Axis Vibration
  • ✓ Comprehensive Non-Destructive Testing: 100% Radiographic (RT), Ultrasonic (UT), Magnetic Particle (MPI), and Dye Penetrant (DPT).
  • ✓ Hydrostatic proof pressure testing up to 1.5x design pressure and helium mass-spectrometer leak testing for zero fugitive emissions.
  • ✓ Environmental climatic chamber stress testing: cyclic thermal shock (-40°C to +85°C) and high-humidity endurance.
  • ✓ Multi-axis shaker table vibration and continuous mechanical shock simulating decades of severe industrial service.
  • ✓ Full-load performance runs verifying flow rates, heat rejection efficiency, thermal saturation, and dynamic control stability.

STAGE 05 // 07 HOMOLOGATION & CERTIFICATION

Homologation & Certification: Statutory Clearances, Third-Party Classification, UN/ECE Type Approvals & Code Stamping

In close collaboration with accredited international technical inspection agencies and regulatory bodies, we provide complete statutory certification, third-party inspection sign-off, and verified Certificates of Conformity (CoC).

ASME U / R Stamp TÜV / DNV / Lloyd's ATEX / IECEx Explosion Proof ECE Type Approvals
  • ✓ ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code (BPVC) compliance, third-party Authorized Inspector (AI) inspection, and U-Stamp documentation.
  • ✓ ATEX and IECEx explosive atmosphere certification for industrial HVAC systems, explosion-proof motors, and hazardous-area skids.
  • ✓ Marine classification society approvals (DNV, Lloyd's Register, ABS) for clean marine propulsion and offshore process equipment.
  • ✓ Automotive UN/ECE Type Approvals (ECE R-100 electrical safety, ECE R-85 net power, ECE R-10 EMC) for road mobility platforms.
  • ✓ Compilation of Manufacturer Data Books (MDB), certified Inspection Test Plans (ITP), material test certificates (3.1/3.2), and CoC dossiers.

Series Production & Turnkey Execution: Heavy Industrial Fabrication, ISO 9001/IATF 16949 Manufacturing & On-Site Plant Construction

Leveraging two decades of industrial execution, Axon operates precision fabrication bays, clean assembly lines, and turnkey site construction teams to deliver complex multi-thousand MT projects and series-produced units under strict quality frameworks.

ISO 9001:2015 & IATF 16949 Heavy Steel Erection (3,000+ MT) 100% End-of-Line QA 2D DataMatrix Traceability
  • ✓ Scalable fabrication and erection: heavy structural steelwork (up to 3,000+ MT), pipe rack pre-fabrication, and plant modularization.
  • ✓ Advanced automated assembly lines with robotic welding, precision torque-angle fastening, and clean-room assembly bays.
  • ✓ Statistical Process Control (SPC) and quality gates ensuring zero-defect manufacturing across every production station.
  • ✓ 100% automated End-of-Line (EOL) factory acceptance testing (FAT): electrical insulation, pneumatic leak integrity, and automated sequence logic.
