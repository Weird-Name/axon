GENERAL R&D PROCESS OVERVIEW

Structured Engineering Across All 7 Stages

Applicable for all research, innovation, and multi-product system realization.

STAGE 01

Concept Stage

We establish the technical foundation of new product and system developments through feasibility studies, requirement analysis, preliminary engineering assessments, and performance modelling to translate concepts into technically viable solutions.

STAGE 02

Design Stage

We deliver integrated engineering design solutions, transforming defined requirements into optimized architectures, detailed technical specifications, engineering drawings, and design configurations, with a focus on functionality, reliability, and performance.

STAGE 03

Prototyping Stage

We facilitate the realization of engineering designs through prototype development, system integration, and functional implementation, enabling design refinement and early-stage assessment of technical feasibility.

STAGE 04

Validation Stage

We execute structured verification and validation activities through engineering analysis, functional testing, and performance assessments to establish compliance with design specifications, operational requirements, and quality benchmarks.

STAGE 05

Homologation Stage

We provide engineering and technical support for product certification and regulatory approval, ensuring conformity with applicable statutory requirements, industry standards, testing protocols, and homologation procedures.

STAGE 06

Series Production

We support the industrialization and production ramp-up of validated designs through manufacturing process development, production optimization, quality assurance, and engineering integration to ensure consistency, scalability, and operational efficiency.

STAGE 07

Aftersales

We deliver lifecycle engineering support through technical diagnostics, field performance analysis, failure investigation, maintenance optimization, and continuous product improvement to enhance reliability, serviceability, and long-term product performance.
