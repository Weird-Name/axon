FLOW METERING

Comprehensive Metering Solutions

Diverse flow meter offerings catering to severe upstream wellheads and transmission networks:

  • Multiphase Flow Meters: Accurately measuring simultaneous oil, water, and gas in challenging oilfield conditions without separation vessels.
  • Custody Transfer: High-integrity fiscal custody transfer measurements meeting strict precision and international standards.
  • Validation & Verification: Guaranteeing the measurement accuracy and data integrity of your metering skids.
  • Calibration & Proving: Precision mobile roving proving services and laboratory recalibration.
  • Software Optimization: Flow computer diagnostic algorithms maximizing operational efficiency.

Telemetry & SCADA Monitoring

Modern Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) systems revolutionizing pipeline monitoring:

  • Automated Routine Control: Replaces manual field checking with autonomous closed loops and automated safety trips.

SPECIALTY REAGENTS

Oilfield Production Chemicals

Advanced production chemicals enhancing operational efficiency and flow assurance:

  • Scale Inhibitors: Controls and prevents salt mineral scale deposition in production tubing and completion strings.
  • Demulsifiers: Rapidly breaks complex emulsions by separating oil from water to optimize separation skids.
