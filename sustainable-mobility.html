SECTION 6 // SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY

## 6. SUSTAINABLE MOBILITY

Engineering the Future of Mobility

Developing next-generation mobility solutions through advanced engineering, electrification and intelligent technologies.

*Key Focus Areas*
FOCUS AREA 01 ROAD ELECTRIFICATION

*Electric Road Mobility:* Electric coaches, buses and passenger transportation

Engineering zero-emission transit solutions for municipal urban bus fleets, intercity luxury passenger coaches, and commercial delivery vehicles. Leveraging 800V silicon carbide (SiC) e-axle architecture and dual-motor propulsion, our platforms deliver ultra-high operating efficiency (>98%), low floor passenger accessibility, and 400+ km daily commercial range.

  • ✓ Electric Passenger Coaches & Transit Buses: Low-entry municipal transit buses and intercity coaches with modular battery capacity up to 450 kWh.
  • ✓ Regenerative Braking Systems: Multi-mode kinetic energy recovery converting over 30% of braking energy back into battery storage during urban stop-and-go cycles.
  • ✓ Heavy Commercial Delivery Platforms: Medium and heavy-duty electric trucks engineered for high payload capacity and zero localized exhaust emissions.
Heavy Electric Coach Powertrain Architecture 800V SiC e-AXLE
FOCUS AREA 02 CLEAN WATERWAYS

*Electric Marine Mobility:* Electric boats, ferries and marine propulsion systems

Transforming coastal and inland water transit with zero-emission electric passenger ferries, harbor workboats, and specialized marine electric propulsion systems. Direct-drive permanent magnet synchronous motors (PMSM) eliminate mechanical gearbox losses, lubricating oil contamination hazards, and engine noise in ecologically sensitive waterways.

  • ✓ Electric Passenger Ferries: High-capacity commercial passenger catamaran vessels delivering silent cruising and up to 80% lower operating costs than diesel.
  • ✓ Electric Harbor Workboats & Tugs: High-torque electric propulsion for port pilot boats, line handlers, and commercial harbor logistics craft.
  • ✓ Turnkey Marine Shore Charging: Automated high-power pontoon and dockside charging connections enabling rapid 15-minute turnaround at passenger terminals.
Direct-Drive Marine Propulsion Assembly ZERO SPILL / PMSM
FOCUS AREA 03 ADVANCED ENERGY SYSTEMS

*Energy Technologies:* Battery integration, charging and energy management

High-density energy storage, active thermal management, and intelligent power conditioning designed for mission-critical duty cycles. Our proprietary liquid-cooled cold plates maintain battery cell temperatures strictly within optimal 25°C–32°C bands even under harsh 50°C tropical ambient conditions, preventing degradation and extending pack lifespan past 4,000 cycles.

  • ✓ Liquid-Cooled Battery Packs: High-density LFP and NMC battery modules featuring laser-welded copper busbars and cell-level fuse protection.
  • ✓ Megawatt Fast-Charging (MCS): DC fast-charging integration up to 1.2 MW supporting rapid opportunity charging for heavy commercial vehicles and ferries.
  • ✓ Smart Energy Management Systems (EMS): Microgrid energy storage (BESS), peak-shaving control, and bidirectional vehicle-to-grid (V2G) power transfer.
Active Liquid-Cooled Thermal Cold Plate 25°C-32°C OPTIMAL
FOCUS AREA 04 DIGITAL INTELLIGENCE

*Intelligent Mobility:* Telematics, remote monitoring and smart control systems

Connected vehicle architectures powered by high-performance Vehicle Control Units (VCU) and secure IoT telematics. Continuous CAN-bus/CAN-FD telemetry streams battery State-of-Health (SoH), motor thermals, and power curves to real-time cloud analytics dashboards with predictive failure detection and over-the-air firmware management.

  • ✓ 24/7 Cloud Fleet Telematics: Comprehensive live telemetry dashboards tracking vehicle location, speed, real-time energy consumption, and operating efficiency.
  • ✓ Predictive Health Monitoring: Artificial intelligence algorithms analyzing cell degradation and component stress to alert operators before failure occurs.
  • ✓ Over-the-Air (OTA) Updates: Secure remote firmware updates, torque curve optimizations, and diagnostic fault clearing without workshop downtime.
VCU Smart Telematics & CAN-Bus Hub CLOUD IoT • OTA
FOCUS AREA 05 DYNAMIC OPTIMIZATION

*Advanced Vehicle Engineering:* Aerodynamics, vehicle architecture and performance optimisation

Combining high-strength structural engineering with multi-physics computer-aided simulation. Utilizing lightweight modular aluminum space-frames and composite structural paneling, our architectures cut tare weight by up to 25% while Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) optimization minimizes aerodynamic drag to maximize driving range.

  • ✓ CFD Aerodynamic Optimization: Aerodynamic body shaping and underbody airflow channeling achieving low drag coefficients (Cd <= 0.32).
  • ✓ Lightweight Vehicle Architectures: High-tensile aluminum space-frames reducing gross vehicle weight and increasing passenger payload capacity.
  • ✓ Crashworthiness & Safety Compliance: Structural FEA simulation validating frontal impact, side intrusion, and rollover protection under UNECE safety standards.
Structural Finite Element Analysis (FEA) LIGHTWEIGHT COMPOSITE
