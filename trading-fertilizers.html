GUARANTEED SPECIFICATION

Guaranteed Composition Profiles

Rigorously audited formulations delivering predictable soil microbiome enrichment:

  • Humic Active Complex (45%–60%): Super-concentrated humic and fulvic matrix engineered to maximize cation exchange capacity (CEC) in depleted soils.
  • Balanced C:N Ratio (< 15:1): Optimized carbon-to-nitrogen ratio ensuring instant microbial bio-stimulation and rapid nitrogen assimilation.
  • Natural Heavy Metals Mitigation: Heavy metal levels tested far below strict international regulatory safety limits, ensuring clean non-toxic runoff.
  • USA Advisory Group Approval: Full formulation compliance with international organic agricultural standards.
ENGINEERED PROCESSING

Advanced Manufacturing Loop

The core manufacturing loop moves entirely away from solid bulk compost, deploying high-precision industrial chemical processing equipment:

  • Multi-Stage High-Shear Blending Reactors: Mechanically breaks down raw biomass to micronized particle sizes.
  • Precision Chelation Vessels: Stabilizes micronutrients (Zinc, Iron, Boron) for complete systemic plant absorption.
  • Stainless Steel Fermentation Tanks: Closed-loop digital temperature and mixing controls ensuring living microbial cultures remain active.
  • 100% Liquid Bio-Nutrients: Stable concentrated solution preventing spray nozzle clogging.
DELIVERY ARCHITECTURE

Precision Foliar Spray & Fertigation

The concentrated liquid formulation allows for instant stomatal uptake and ultra-flexible commercial agricultural deployment:

  • Micro-Drip Fertigation Systems: Dissolves seamlessly without sediment buildup, delivering bio-nutrients directly to root zones.
  • Tractor-Mounted Wide-Boom Sprayers: High-acreage uniform coverage for broadacre cereals and row crops.
  • Precision Agricultural UAV Drones: Uncrewed aerial vehicle misting optimizing chemical usage and reaching steep orchard terrains.
