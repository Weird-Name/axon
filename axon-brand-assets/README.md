### 3. Usage Guidelines
* Always maintain the original aspect ratio when resizing.
* Use the transparent PNG or SVG files on white, light grey, or dark navy corporate backgrounds.
* For print materials (visiting cards, brochures, equipment nameplates), use `axon-icon-master.svg` or `axon-logo-full-2000px.png`.
