Corporate Privacy Policy

How Axon Petro Engineering Pvt. Ltd. collects, manages, and safeguards proprietary engineering data, partner disclosures, and website visitor communications.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Corporate Commitment to Privacy

Axon Petro Engineering Pvt. Ltd. ("Axon", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our clients, suppliers, joint-venture partners, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details our protocols regarding the collection, processing, protection, and retention of personal and technical data submitted through our website (axonpetro.in).

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide industrial engineering services, technical consultations, and business communications:

  • Technical Inquiry Data: Full name, professional corporate email address, corporate telephone number, organization/company name, and engineering project requirements submitted through our contact forms or direct email inquiries.
  • Vendor Pre-Qualification Disclosures: Information provided by procurement auditors, EPC contractors, and materials suppliers for vetting against ASME, AWS, and ISO statutory standards.
  • Technical Web Analytics: Non-personally identifiable log files, browser type, device operating system, referring URL, and page interaction timestamps used solely for website performance optimization and security audits.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your information under legitimate business interests and pre-contractual execution frameworks to:

  • Respond to technical RFQs, project specification inquiries, and commercial proposals.
  • Provide access to certified procedure documentation (e.g., Welding Procedure Specifications registers).
  • Coordinate joint engineering evaluations, feasibility assessments, and NDA execution.
  • Comply with applicable legal, statutory, and regulatory engineering obligations.

4. Data Confidentiality & Proprietary Engineering Information

Axon treats all client engineering specifications, 3D CAD models, process duty parameters, and metallurgical datasheets with the highest degree of confidentiality. Technical project data is never sold, leased, or distributed to third parties for commercial marketing purposes. Data is accessed strictly by authorized engineers and technical officers on a need-to-know basis.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your personal or project data with external entities except:

  • Strategic Joint Ventures: With authorized representatives of our strategic partner Greengold Energy (JV) when necessary to formulate turnkey project proposals.
  • Statutory & Inspection Authorities: Authorized third-party inspection bodies (e.g., ASME Authorized Inspectors, TÜV, Lloyd's Register) where mandated for code compliance and equipment certification.
  • Legal Obligations: When strictly required by law, subpoena, or competent regulatory authority.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard technical and organizational security controls—including TLS/HTTPS encryption, secure firewalls, and restricted administrative access—to safeguard information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Commercial inquiry records are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the business or technical purpose for which they were collected.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable data protection regulations (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act and international privacy frameworks), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by Axon. Inquiries regarding your data may be directed to our compliance desk at projects@axonpetro.in.
