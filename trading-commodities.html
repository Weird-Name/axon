Comprehensive Metering Solutions

Diverse flow meter offerings catering to severe upstream and midstream operational environments:

  • Multiphase Flow Meters: Accurately measuring simultaneous oil, water, and gas flows under harsh, turbulent wellhead conditions without preliminary phase separation.
  • Custody Transfer Metering: High-precision fiscal custody transfer systems meeting strict API MPMS, OIML, and AGA accuracy standards.
  • Validation & Verification: Field proving and audit services guaranteeing system measurement integrity.
  • Calibration & Roving Proving: Periodic recalibration, master meter verification, and mobile roving proving services.
  • Software Optimization: Diagnostic metering analytics and flow computer integration maximizing measurement uptime.

Oilfield Production Chemicals

Specialty formulations enhancing operational flow assurance and hydrocarbon separation efficiency:

  • Scale Inhibitors: Formulated to control and prevent salt mineral scale deposition in production tubing and completion strings, maintaining unrestricted flow.
  • Demulsifiers: Rapidly destabilizes and breaks complex water-in-oil emulsions, accelerating phase separation in dehydration skids.
  • Pour Point Depressants (PPD): Prevents formation of wax paraffin crystals in crude oil, lowering pour points for flowability in cold ambient pipelines.

SUB-VERTICAL 02 USA ADVISORY APPROVED • LIQUID BIO-NUTRIENTS

Organic and Chemical Fertilizers

Native Indian manufacturer of 100% verified organic fertilizers fully reviewed and approved by USA advisory groups. Deploying advanced biological processing chains to formulate dense, bio-available liquid nutrients instead of raw composted waste.

Agronomic Specification Sheet
GUARANTEED SPECIFICATION

Guaranteed Composition Profiles

  • Humic Active Complex (45%–60%): Super-concentrated humic and fulvic matrix engineered to maximize cation exchange capacity (CEC) in depleted soils.
  • Balanced C:N Ratio (< 15:1): Optimized carbon-to-nitrogen ratio ensuring instant microbial bio-stimulation and rapid nitrogen assimilation.
  • Natural Heavy Metals Mitigation: Heavy metal levels tested far below strict international regulatory safety limits, ensuring clean non-toxic runoff.
  • USA Advisory Group Approval: Full formulation compliance with international organic agricultural standards.
ENGINEERED PROCESSING

Advanced Manufacturing Loop

The core manufacturing loop moves entirely away from solid bulk compost, deploying multi-stage high-shear blending reactors, chelation vessels, and sanitary stainless steel fermentation tanks.

Closed-loop digital temperature and mixing controls break down premium organic feedstocks into concentrated, stable liquid bio-nutrients with complete batch consistency.

APPLICATION MODALITIES

Precision Foliar Spray & Fertigation

The concentrated liquid formulation allows for instant stomatal uptake and ultra-flexible commercial agricultural deployment:

  • Micro-Drip Fertigation: Non-clogging formula delivering nutrients straight to the root zone.
  • Tractor Boom Field Sprayers: High-acreage uniform coverage.
  • Agricultural Spraying UAV Drones: Precision aerial foliage misting.
